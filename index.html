Built for the way groups play

Simple on the course. Surprisingly deep after the round.

Golf apps track your game. Mulligan Crew keeps the group together—from the first tee to the season table and the trip story.

Live group scoring

Score the whole group.

Search real courses and tee sets, choose nine or eighteen holes, and score up to four golfers from one phone. Start on Hole 1 or Hole 10 when the tee sheet demands it.

  • Fast hole-by-hole entryNet and gross standings stay visible without turning scoring into a spreadsheet.
  • Bring everyone inOther phones join by QR or short code and follow the same accepted scorecard.
  • Guests are welcomeAdd a golfer before they have an account. Their identity and history can be claimed later.
  • Built for weak serviceThe scorekeeper’s card saves on the phone and finishes publishing after the connection returns.

One shared scorecard. The scorer enters the facts; the group stays with the round.

The Crew clubhouse

Turn regular rounds into a season.

Crew Cup uses hole-by-hole net Stableford so mixed skill levels can share one season without pretending everyone plays the same game.

  • Best five + your finaleEach golfer’s best five qualifying regular results count, followed by one personal Championship round.
  • Your group’s playing numberCrew HCP distributes group-agreed extra strokes by hole difficulty. It is not an official Handicap Index.
  • More than a standings tableKeep crowns, category leaders, completed rounds, and the stories a scorecard normally loses.

Competition with continuity—standings now, permanent Crew history later.

Golf trips

Run the whole golf trip.

Plan the dates, courses, tees, tee times, groups, and scorekeepers before anyone reaches the first tee. Then bring the results back to one Trip dashboard.

  • Every tee time has a homeOrganize multiple foursomes and assign a scorekeeper to each planned round.
  • One optional finaleTag separate eighteen-hole groups as the Championship. Each golfer appears once, and every group feeds one combined field.
  • A Trip record, not a dead itineraryKeep completed rounds, standings, notes, gross recognition, Moments, and the final recap together.

Separate tee times. Separate scorers. One shared Trip and one optional finale.

Our story

Built by a firefighter for his own golf crew.

Seth Theroux created Mulligan Crew after watching his group’s best rounds, running jokes, and annual trips disappear into paper scorecards and group texts. The app was refined around how his own Crew actually plays: one scorekeeper, golfers of every level, and a clubhouse everyone can return to.

The goal is simple: make live scoring easy, then give the group something lasting when the round is over.
