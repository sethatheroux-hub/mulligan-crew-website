How can we help?

Support for rounds, live joining, Crews, Seasons, Trips, synchronization, and your account.

Contact Mulligan Crew support mulligancrewsupport@gmail.com

A completed round is waiting to submit

Reconnect to Wi-Fi or cellular service, open Mulligan Crew, and use the retry action on Home. Keep the app installed until the round confirms that it has been submitted.

Join a live round

Tap Join on Home and scan the scorekeeper's QR code or enter the private join code. Live following needs a connection, but the scorekeeper can still record a golfer who has not downloaded the app.

Take over scoring

Open Join code & round devices from the live round, request the transfer, and have the current scorekeeper approve it. Wait for both phones to confirm before continuing.

Export or delete your account

Open Me → Privacy & account. You can export your account data or start account deletion there. Email support if you cannot access the app.

What to include in a support message

Tell us the app version, whether you were online or offline, and what you expected to happen. Screenshots help. Never send your password, private access token, or a scannable active join code.
